I'm Michiel Bakker. For over 10 years, I've built cloud platforms and Kubernetes infrastructure that runs in production — for large enterprises, startups, and everything in between.

My expertise: **Google Cloud Platform**, **Kubernetes**, and **DevOps transformation**. Building scalable, robust systems is what I'm passionate about, always with a close eye on data security.

**What I work on:** cloud migrations, platform architecture, CI/CD hardening, and new applications of artificial intelligence in our field.
